Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Find quick answers about CarbonIQ AI.

What is CarbonIQ AI?

CarbonIQ AI is an AI-powered carbon emission prediction platform designed to help textile industries monitor, analyze, and reduce carbon emissions efficiently.

How does CarbonIQ AI calculate carbon emissions?

The platform uses production data, electricity consumption, fuel usage, water consumption, and transportation details to estimate carbon emissions using Artificial Intelligence.

Can I monitor emissions in real time?

Yes. CarbonIQ AI provides real-time monitoring, historical analysis, and AI-based predictions through an interactive dashboard.

Can I download emission reports?

Absolutely. Users can generate and download detailed carbon emission reports in PDF format for documentation and analysis.

Will I receive alerts if emissions exceed the limit?

Yes. CarbonIQ AI notifies users whenever the predicted carbon emission exceeds the predefined safety threshold.
